How an answer is produced

  1. 01

    Chunk

    Documents are split by heading into chunks of about 140 words, with overlap so facts are not cut in half.

  2. 02

    Rank

    BM25 with a heading boost and synonym expansion selects the top 5 chunks in about a millisecond.

  3. 03

    Check

    A confidence score tests whether the chunks cover the question. Below the threshold, it declines.

  4. 04

    Answer

    Gemini writes a grounded answer citing [1][2]. Without a key, an extractive composer quotes the passages.
