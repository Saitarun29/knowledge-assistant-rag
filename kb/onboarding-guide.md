# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Labs! This guide walks you through your first 30 days.

## Before day one

- Your laptop (MacBook Pro by default; Linux ThinkPad on request) ships to your home address 5 business days before your start date.
- You will receive an invitation to your Google Workspace account at your personal email. Accept it and set up 2-step verification.
- Complete the I-9 / right-to-work check in BambooHR.

## Day one (your first day)

- **09:30** — Welcome session with the People Team (Zoom link in your calendar).
- **11:00** — IT setup: install Okta Verify, enroll the laptop in Kandji (our device management), and sign in to Slack.
- **14:00** — Lunch with your onboarding buddy. The company covers the meal.
- **16:00** — 1:1 with your manager to agree on your 30/60/90-day plan.

## Accounts you'll get

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Google Workspace | Email, calendar, docs |
| Slack | Day-to-day communication |
| Notion | Wiki and team pages |
| Linear | Engineering & product tickets |
| HubSpot | CRM (Sales and CS only) |
| BambooHR | Leave, payslips, personal data |
| Expensify | Expenses |

Access to production systems is granted only after you finish the security training (see the Security Policy).

## First week

- Complete the mandatory **Security Awareness** and **Code of Conduct** courses in Lessonly within **7 days**.
- Join #general, #announcements, #team-ooo and your team channel.
- Read the product FAQ and book a 30-minute product demo with Solutions Engineering.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 90 days. Meet at least weekly. Buddies are your go-to for "where do I find…" questions.

## 30 / 60 / 90 days

- **30 days:** ship your first small contribution and meet every person on your team.
- **60 days:** own one recurring responsibility.
- **90 days:** probation review with your manager. Probation is 90 days for all roles.

## Equipment returns

If you leave Northwind, return your laptop within 10 days using the prepaid box IT sends you.
