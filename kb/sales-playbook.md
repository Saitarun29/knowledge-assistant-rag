# Sales Playbook

How the Northwind Labs sales team finds, qualifies and closes customers for Northwind Flow.

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

- Companies with **200–5,000 employees** in SaaS, fintech, logistics and healthcare.
- Operations, RevOps or IT team that owns business processes.
- Uses at least 3 of our top connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, NetSuite, Slack).

## Qualification — MEDDICC

Every opportunity above $20k ARR must have MEDDICC fields filled in HubSpot before moving to the Proposal stage: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition.

## Pipeline stages

1. **Discovery** — first call, pain identified.
2. **Demo** — tailored demo with a Solutions Engineer.
3. **Technical validation** — proof of concept, max **14 days**.
4. **Proposal** — pricing sent.
5. **Negotiation** — legal and procurement.
6. **Closed won / lost**.

## Lead response

Inbound demo requests must be contacted within **1 business hour**. Leads are routed round-robin by region in HubSpot. If an inbound lead is not contacted within 24 hours, it is reassigned.

## Handling common objections

- **"It's too expensive."** Anchor on time saved: customers automate on average 120 hours of manual work per month. Offer annual billing (20% off) before discounting.
- **"We already use Zapier."** Position Flow for governed, high-volume workflows: SSO, audit logs, data residency and an SLA.
- **"Security will block it."** Share the SOC 2 Type II report via the Trust Center after an NDA is signed.

## Discounts and approvals

Reps can approve up to 15% discount. 15–30% needs Sales Director approval, above 30% needs CFO approval. Never discount in the first call.

## Commission

Account Executives earn **10% of first-year ACV**, paid on cash collected. Multi-year deals earn an additional 2% on year two.

## Handover to Customer Success

Within **2 business days** of Closed Won, the AE schedules a handover call with the CSM and fills in the handover form in HubSpot (goals, stakeholders, integrations, risks).
