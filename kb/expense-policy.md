# Expense & Reimbursement Policy

Northwind Labs reimburses reasonable, business-related expenses. Spend company money as if it were your own.

## How to submit

1. Submit every expense in **Expensify** within **30 days** of the purchase date.
2. Attach an itemised receipt for any expense over **$25**.
3. Add the cost center and a one-line business purpose.
4. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the **next payroll cycle** (payroll runs on the 25th).

Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval limits

| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Your manager |
| $500 – $5,000 | Department head |
| Over $5,000 | CFO, plus a purchase order raised in advance |

## Travel

- **Flights:** economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights over 6 hours. Book through Navan at least 14 days ahead where possible.
- **Hotels:** up to **$220 per night** in tier-1 cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Singapore) and **$160 per night** elsewhere.
- **Meals:** daily per diem of **$75** while travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.
- **Ground transport:** rideshare and public transport are fine; car rentals need manager approval.

## Client entertainment

Client meals are capped at **$120 per attendee**. List every attendee and their company in Expensify. Gifts to clients may not exceed $100 per person per year.

## Home office & learning

- One-time **home office stipend of $500** for new hires (desk, chair, monitor).
- **Learning budget of $1,000 per year** for courses, books and conferences. Get manager approval before booking a conference.
- Internet is reimbursed up to **$40 per month** for remote employees.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than once per quarter can request a Ramp corporate card. Personal spending on a corporate card is not allowed; accidental charges must be repaid within 7 days.

## Not reimbursable

Fines and parking tickets, personal entertainment, upgrades you chose for comfort, and lost personal items.
