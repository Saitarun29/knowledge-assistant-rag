# HR Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time Northwind Labs employees. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis. Contractors are governed by their individual agreements.

## Annual (paid) leave

Every full-time employee receives **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Leave can be taken from your first day; you may go up to 5 days negative in your first year.

Up to **8 unused days carry over** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, after which they expire. Unused leave is not paid out except where local law requires it.

Requests of 3 days or more should be submitted in BambooHR at least **2 weeks in advance**. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. During the last two weeks of each quarter, Finance and Sales leave requires director approval.

## Sick leave

Employees get **12 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry over. Notify your manager and post in #team-ooo before 10:00 local time on the day. Absences longer than **3 consecutive days** require a medical certificate uploaded to BambooHR.

## Parental leave

- Primary caregivers: **26 weeks fully paid**.
- Secondary caregivers: **6 weeks fully paid**, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
- Employees become eligible after 6 months of service.
- A phased return (60% hours at full pay) is available for the first 4 weeks back.

## Other leave

- **Bereavement:** 5 days for immediate family, 2 days for extended family.
- **Marriage:** 3 days, once during employment.
- **Volunteering:** 2 days per year with a registered non-profit.
- **Sabbatical:** after 5 years of service, employees may take 4 weeks of paid sabbatical.

## Public holidays

Northwind observes the public holidays of the country in which you are employed. The holiday calendar for each office is published in Notion under *People > Holidays*. If you work on a public holiday at your manager's request, you receive a compensatory day off within 60 days.
