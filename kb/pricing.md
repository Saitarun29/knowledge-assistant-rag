# Pricing & Plans

Prices are in USD per month and apply to Northwind Flow.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Included tasks / month | Users | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49 | 5,000 | Up to 3 | Core connectors, email support |
| Business | $299 | 50,000 | Up to 25 | All connectors, AI Step, SSO, chat support |
| Enterprise | Custom (from $1,500) | Custom | Unlimited | Data residency, 99.95% SLA, HIPAA BAA, CSM |

A "task" is one successful action performed by a workflow step.

## Annual billing

Annual billing gives a **20% discount** on Starter and Business. Enterprise contracts are annual by default, with multi-year terms available.

## Overages

Extra tasks are billed at **$10 per additional 1,000 tasks** on Starter and **$6 per 1,000** on Business. Customers are notified by email at 80% and 100% of their quota. Workflows are never paused for overages on Business or Enterprise.

## Discounts

- **Non-profits and education:** 40% off Business.
- **Startups** (under 2 years old, under $5M raised): 50% off Business for the first year through the Northwind Startup Program.
- Sales reps may offer up to **15% discount** on their own. Discounts of 15–30% require Sales Director approval; anything above 30% needs CFO approval.

## Payment terms

Self-serve plans are paid by card. Enterprise invoices are **Net 30**. We accept ACH, wire transfer and card.

## Cancellations and refunds

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the billing period. Annual plans can be refunded pro-rata within the **first 30 days**; after that, no refunds are issued.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
