# Product FAQ — Northwind Flow

Northwind Flow is our workflow automation platform. It lets operations teams connect their tools, automate approvals and sync data without writing code.

## What does Northwind Flow do?

Flow connects business apps (CRMs, helpdesks, spreadsheets, databases) and runs automated workflows triggered by events, schedules or webhooks. Typical uses: lead routing, invoice approvals, customer onboarding and data syncs.

## Which integrations are supported?

Flow ships with **180+ native connectors**, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Slack, Google Sheets, NetSuite, Stripe, Postgres and Snowflake. Any REST API can be called with the generic HTTP connector.

## Where is customer data hosted?

Data is hosted on AWS in **us-east-1 (Virginia)** by default. Enterprise customers can choose **eu-central-1 (Frankfurt)** or **ap-south-1 (Mumbai)** for data residency.

## Is Flow secure and compliant?

Yes. Flow is **SOC 2 Type II** certified and GDPR compliant. Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). A HIPAA BAA is available on the Enterprise plan.

## What is the uptime SLA?

Business plan: **99.9%** monthly uptime. Enterprise plan: **99.95%** with service credits. Live status is published at status.northwindlabs.example.

## Can workflows use AI?

Yes. The **AI Step** lets workflows classify, summarise or extract data using an LLM. Customers can bring their own OpenAI or Gemini key, or use Northwind's managed model credits.

## Is there an API?

Yes, a REST API with API-key and OAuth 2.0 authentication. Rate limit is **600 requests per minute** per workspace on Business and **3,000** on Enterprise.

## Is there a free trial?

A **14-day free trial** of the Business plan is available with no credit card required.

## How do customers get support?

Starter: email support, 2 business-day response. Business: chat and email, 8-hour response. Enterprise: 24/7 support with a **1-hour response for P1 issues** and a named Customer Success Manager.
